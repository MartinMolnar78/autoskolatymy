## Použitý prompt

Use case: compositing / background-extraction. Create a premium photorealistic automotive website hero asset by extracting and composing BOTH actual Hyundai i20 driving-school cars from the two supplied photographs. Image 1 is the lime green Hyundai facing front-right, Image 2 is the white Hyundai facing front-left. Preserve their identities, original proportions, body shapes, lime and white paint colors, black roofs, Hyundai badges, wheels and AUTOŠKOLA roof signs; retain the supplied Tymy driving school door branding accurately and do not invent new wording. Remove buildings, trees, asphalt, cones and all original backgrounds. Compose them together in a landscape 3:2 canvas: WHITE car at upper right and slightly smaller behind, GREEN car prominent in foreground lower left/center facing right. Keep enough separation to read BOTH entire cars, all wheels and roof signs fully inside frame, tasteful overlap of green over white less than 12 percent. Cars occupy 90 percent of frame width and 75 percent height, 5 percent margin on all sides. Match natural soft studio lighting with crisp clean reflections and subdued soft contact shadows under tires. Background must be genuinely transparent with alpha, including through all negative space. No road graphics, text blocks, numbers, badges, extra logos or scene; these will be added in HTML/CSS. Only two realistic original vehicles and their soft ground shadows. This is a cutout marketing composite for a cream/red/yellow modern website, not a website mockup. Do not change car model or add a third car.
